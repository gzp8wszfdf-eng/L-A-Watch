Privacy Policy — L&A Watch

Last updated: November 5, 2026

L&A Watch is a watch-together service that lets users create or join rooms and use supported communication and media features. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use the service.

Information we process

  • Account information: information supplied by your sign-in provider, such as your email address and basic profile details, to authenticate your account.
  • Service content: room details, chat messages, and other information you choose to submit or share through the service.
  • Media and device permissions: audio/video or selected media only when you choose to use features that require microphone, camera, or file access. You can control these permissions in your device or browser settings.
  • YouTube account information: if you choose to connect YouTube, the service requests the YouTube Data API read-only permission to retrieve basic information about your YouTube channel. The service does not use this permission to upload, edit, or delete YouTube content.
  • Technical information: information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service, which may be processed by our hosting and infrastructure providers.

How information is used

Information is used to provide sign-in, rooms, communication, media features you request, maintain security, and troubleshoot the service. We do not sell personal information.

Third-party services

The service may rely on Google and YouTube APIs for authentication or YouTube features, Supabase for supported account and application data, and Cloudflare for hosting and delivery. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. YouTube API Services are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

Data retention and security

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably needed to provide and secure the service, or as otherwise required by law. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may stop using the service, disconnect a connected Google/YouTube account where that option is provided, and revoke access through your Google Account permissions. You can also manage microphone and other device permissions in your browser or operating system.

Children

L&A Watch is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. For privacy questions, contact farcry4rent@gmail.com.
